RDS Cloud Kitchen Standard Operating, Food Safety & Delivery Disclaimers

1. Hyperlocal Delivery Limit & Fixed Point Policy

Orders are dispatched exclusively within our automated 3.0 KM geographic boundary to ensure food arrives at optimal temperature. If the kitchen activates a "Fixed Delivery Point" for residential quarters or campuses, handovers take place strictly at the designated gate or landmark.

2. Delivery Failure & Unforeseen Delays

The kitchen is not liable for failed or delayed fulfillment caused by incorrect phone numbers, unanswered doorbells, inaccurate GPS pins, severe weather, regional road blockades, or gate access denial by security. Re-dispatch is not possible once food leaves the facility.

3. Food Safety, Quality & Allergens

All items are prepared in a cloud kitchen environment that processes milk, gluten, tree nuts, legumes, and traditional spices. For food safety and flavor retention, consume within 60 minutes of delivery.

4. Payment Settlements & Cancellations

We accept verified online UPI QR/phone transfers and Cash on Delivery (COD). Online transactions must be settled immediately upon placement. Once prep commences or the ordering cutoff passes, cancellations and refunds cannot be entertained.

5. Meal Slot Timers & Surplus Stock Notice

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Pre-Orders each adhere to rigid daily cutoff times. Dishes displayed during surplus food flash announcements represent fresh, limited-quantity excess batches sold on a strict first-come, first-served basis.
